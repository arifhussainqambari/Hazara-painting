Our Story

Built on a decade of doing it properly.

Hazara Painting & Decorating is owner-operated by Arif Hussain Qambari — a painter with more than ten years on the tools across Melbourne. When you call Hazara, you speak directly with the person who will stand in your hallway, cut your edges, and sign off on the final coat. No call centres, no handballing.

Every job runs on the same three rules: the highest quality paints and materials, precise technique — brush, roller or spray — and a price that's honest from the first quote to the final invoice.

The Craft

Three tools. One obsession.

— 01

Brush

Crisp cutting-in, clean lines around trims and ceilings, and detail work machines can't touch. The difference between painted and finished.

— 02

Roller

Even, consistent coverage on walls and ceilings with the right nap for every surface — smooth sets, textured renders, everything between.

— 03

Spray

Factory-grade finishes on doors, cabinetry and large commercial areas — fast, flawless and impossible to replicate by hand.
